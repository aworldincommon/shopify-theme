# World in Common Shopify Theme Design Contract

## Brand

World in Common is a media, research, design, and product project about building systems that help people belong. It explores human-scale life, global solidarity, meaningful work, monetary systems, shared value, open tools, and practical alternatives to isolation and concentrated power.

The storefront should feel like an independent political magazine, underground cafe, and high-quality streetwear label: warm, intelligent, slightly sarcastic, constructive, and materially grounded.

## Visual system

- Ink black: `#111111`
- Warm paper: `#F4F0E8`
- Newsprint: `#E7E0D2`
- Deep burgundy: `#A3161A`
- Army green: `#4A5233`
- Rules: black at low opacity

Burgundy leads the palette: the nameplate, primary buttons, stamps, rules, labels, every second topic, the announcement bar, the ledger band and the footer. Keep paper and newsprint sections between burgundy bands so the page does not read as one red wash.

Typography: Anton (condensed poster face, uppercase) for display headings; Archivo for body copy; monospace only for receipt/ledger metadata.

Emblems: the two approved screen-print marks, #1 brick arch and #2 square seal, live in `assets/wic-emblem-arch.webp` and `assets/wic-emblem-roof.webp` with transparent backgrounds. Place them on paper or newsprint only, or on a paper card on darker bands. Their black and burgundy parts disappear on the ink and burgundy schemes.

Graphic language: thermal receipts, torn paper, rubber stamps, ledger rules, photocopy grain, screen-print imperfections, generous whitespace, and strong asymmetrical editorial composition.

## Do

- Let one idea land per section.
- Use real assets when supplied; otherwise use restrained placeholders.
- Keep copy concise and human.
- Show TBD or awaiting-real-costs instead of invented prices.
- Make product and checkout paths clear.
- Preserve Dawn's accessibility, responsive behavior, and Shopify conventions.

## Don't

- No stock-photo hero.
- No gradients, glassmorphism, crypto imagery, flags, globe icons, generic sustainability symbols, or corporate nonprofit styling.
- No invented prices, statistics, testimonials, suppliers, or private contact details.
- No fake slogans or corrupted generated typography.
- Avoid the word `extract` in public-facing copy where a clearer phrase works.
