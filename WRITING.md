# World in Common: Writing Voice

These rules apply to everything we publish: the website, essays, posts, product copy, emails, and videos. Read this before writing or editing any public copy.

## The core rule: affirm, then invite

People who find us are often already doing something: recycling, volunteering, voting, reading, worrying, showing up. **We never tell them it isn't enough, and we never tell them their feelings are wrong.**

We say: *what you're doing matters. Let's do more, together.*

- Meet people where they are. Acknowledge the effort, then open the next door.
- Validate the feeling (frustration, exhaustion, anger, hope) before offering a direction.
- Invite rather than instruct. Use "let's" and "we" more than "you should".
- Use agency framing: take matters into our own hands, build, try, share, start small.

## Aim the criticism at systems, not people

- Criticize institutions, incentives, and concentrated power. Never criticize the reader.
- Sarcasm and provocation are welcome, but they punch up, at systems and power, and never at the audience.
- Don't assume bad intent in people who disagree. Most people want human flourishing; they disagree on how and when.

## Words and framings we avoid

| Avoid | Why | Prefer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Stop complaining", "less complaining" | Dismisses real feelings | "It's time to take matters into our own hands." |
| "That's not enough", "you're not doing enough" | Shames existing effort | "What you're doing matters. Let's build on it." |
| "Wake up", "sheep", "you're part of the problem" | Contempt for the reader | "We can't keep trusting institutions to fix this for us." |
| "Just" as in "just buying stuff", "just posting" | Belittles | Name the action without judging it |
| "Extract", "extractive" | Jargon | "Concentrate value in the hands of a few", "take more than they give" |
| "Third World" | Hierarchical | "Global South", or name the region |
| "Profit" for surplus | Misleading | "Operating surplus", "contribution to the project" |
| Savior or hero framing | Puts us above the reader | We're learning too; we're starting small |

## Tone

- Young, warm, intelligent, slightly sarcastic, hopeful without being naive.
- Humble. We don't have all the answers, and we say so.
- Honest. No invented numbers, quotes, testimonials, or claims. Write "TBD" when something isn't known yet.
- Plain language. Short sentences. Statements, not slogans.

## Quick self-check before publishing

1. Would someone who's already trying feel respected after reading this?
2. Is any criticism aimed at a system rather than a person?
3. Does it end with an invitation or a next step?
4. Is every fact real and every number sourced?
